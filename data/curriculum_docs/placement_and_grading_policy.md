# Sprints Placement and Grading Policy
## Readiness statuses
- Ready for Placement: completed at least 12 modules and Python assignment score of 80 or above.
- In Progress: learner is still completing modules.
- Needs Review: low assignment score or few completed modules; a human advisor must review the case.
- Dropped: learner left the bootcamp and cannot be placed.

## Grading rubric for Python assignments
Correctness 50 percent (hidden tests), code quality 20 percent (naming, docstrings), error handling 20 percent, clarity of feedback 10 percent. Passing score is 70.

## Placement approval
Employer partners: Fawry, Vodafone Egypt, e& Egypt, and Swvl. Only a human advisor may approve a placement. The AI prepares a recommendation with the learner's readiness, score, and target salary in EGP, then escalates for human approval.
